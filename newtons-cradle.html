Objective

Launch 1 ball to 24°

Waiting for release ±0°

Controls

  • Drag the highlighted group on the left to set the release angle.
  • Keyboard: focus the cradle, use Right or Up to pull farther, Left or Down to ease off, then press Space or Enter to release.
  • Release to send the same number of balls swinging out on the right.
  • Perfect rebounds boost your streak and earn bigger score multipliers.

Scoring

  • Perfect: within 2° of target.
  • Great: within 5° of target.
  • Good: within 9° of target.
  • Misses break the streak, but you can retry the same round.

Physics note

This is a stylized cradle: momentum transfer and damping are tuned to feel clean and readable instead of perfectly simulating every collision.

One self-contained HTML file. No assets, no libraries, no setup.
