Bughouse Tutor

How bughouse works, and the rules this page uses
  • Two boards, two teams of two. Partners sit side by side and play opposite colours.
  • Every piece you capture is handed to your partner. It goes into their reserve, shown beside their board.
  • On your turn you may drop a reserve piece on any empty square instead of moving. Tap the piece in your reserve, then the square.
  • Pawns may not be dropped on the 1st or 8th rank. A dropped piece may give check or checkmate, and may block a check.
  • A promoted pawn that gets captured goes back to being a pawn.
  • Checkmate on either board ends the match for both boards.
  • There is no clock here so you have time to read the tutor. The two boards take turns, one move each. At a real tournament both boards run at once on clocks.
  • Here it is checkmate when you have no legal move with the pieces you hold now. Some tournaments let a player wait for a partner's piece to block; check your event's rules, and whether drop mates are allowed.
