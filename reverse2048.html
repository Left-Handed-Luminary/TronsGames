Reverse Puzzle

2048 To Zero

Split the board downward from giant powers of two to a final pair of 2s. Tap a tile, then swipe or choose a direction to send one half into an empty neighbor while the score counts down to zero.

Goal

Reach exactly 0 score and leave only two 2-tiles on the board.

Extra 2-tiles are automatically banked away so you can keep carving deeper chains without clogging the grid.

Controls

  • Tap a tile to open the split compass.
  • Swipe after selecting a tile to split in that direction.
  • Undo Reverse restores the previous board state.
  • Hint highlights a safe depth-first split.

Mode Notes

Pure Mode starts from one classic 2048 root worth 20,480 reverse points.

Max Mode opens with a chaotic bonus layout and a massive 82,884 score.
