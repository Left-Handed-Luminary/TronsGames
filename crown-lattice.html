How to play
  • Place exactly one crown in each row, each column, and each color region.
  • Crowns may not touch, not even corner to corner.
  • Use X marks to rule cells out. They never count against you.
  • Rule breaks glow red right away.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Space/Enter tap, X mark, Q crown, Del clear, U or Ctrl+Z undo, H hint.
  • Mouse: right-click toggles an X.
